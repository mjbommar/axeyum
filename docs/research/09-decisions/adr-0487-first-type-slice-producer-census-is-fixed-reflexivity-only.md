# ADR-0487: First type-slice producer census is fixed reflexivity only

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-08-19
Index-summary: Run exactly the existing bounded Pi-equality reflexivity producer once on every checked train/development slice, preserving structured declines and zero admission authority

## Context

ADR-0486 and the exact-commit binder replay establish proof-free producer
boundaries for all 138 frozen Mathlib train/development statements. The next
flywheel arrow must measure proof construction rather than adapter reachability.
That measurement needs to be frozen before its new outcomes are observed;
otherwise budgets or routing could be widened until the corpus appears solved.

The only already-settled generic producer for imported surface statements is
ADR-0481's Pi-wrapped equality reflexivity grammar. Induction operations are
currently exact or catalog-bound to the Autogenesis-1 Nat chain, so presenting
them as generic coverage would confuse operation authority with capability.

## Decision

The first complete type-slice producer census runs exactly
`bounded-pi-equality-reflexivity-v1` once on each of the 138 checked
train/development slices under its existing limits of eight Pi binders and
sixteen constructed expression nodes.

For every row, the driver must:

1. reproduce the v3 checked type slice and its content-addressed receipt;
2. hand only the independently imported generalized goal and proof-free fresh
   kernel to the producer;
3. record a stable producer decline, kernel rejection, assurance rejection, or
   admissible proof outcome;
4. type-check every proposed term as a transient theorem in that fresh kernel;
5. require zero axioms, zero theorem dependencies, and no dependency on the
   transparent goal definition; and
6. perform zero fact-ledger writes and grant no operation or admission authority.

The population, producer identity, budgets, row order, source mapping, type-slice
policy, and outcome taxonomy are fixed as
`type-slice-reflexivity-census-v1`. Held-out rows, upstream proof bodies, target
outcomes, alternate producers, retries, adaptive budgets, and per-family tuning
remain unavailable. A checker must fail on completion alone unless all 138 rows
have one receipt and one classified producer outcome.

After the census, structured declines may select the next reusable producer or
representation capability. The v1 result itself may not be rerun with broader
budgets under the same policy identity.

## Evidence

- The exact binder replay accepts 138/138 train/development rows, with 128 exact
  receipts and ten checked-normalization receipts.
- ADR-0481 already fixes the proposed grammar, budgets, independent kernel
  check, and dependency audit and includes unequal-side and budget controls.
- The earlier pre-slice coverage separated 114 adapter rejections, fifteen
  producer declines, seven kernel rejections, and two admissible proofs. It
  demonstrated why adapter outcomes must not be reported as proof-search yield.
- No generic induction or solver-backed imported-goal producer currently has a
  settled all-population input and evidence contract.

## Alternatives

- **Route each family to its hypothesized producer.** Rejected because route
  hypotheses were frozen for evaluation stratification, not granted operation
  authority, and would expose different budgets per family.
- **Add reflexivity and induction before measuring.** Rejected because a
  multi-producer result would not localize which reusable capability changed
  yield, while current induction drivers are exact-chain operations.
- **Raise the binder budget for generalized goals.** Rejected for v1 because
  abstraction-induced budget pressure is itself a useful structured outcome.
- **Open held-out after reaching 138/138 adapter coverage.** Rejected because
  the proof policy and its capability-development loop have not yet been frozen
  through train/development evidence.

## Consequences

The next measurement is intentionally narrow but honest: it establishes the
first full conversion rate from checked goal boundary to independently accepted
proof under one preregistered grammar. It will likely produce many declines;
those declines become the demand signal for the next flywheel turn. Successes
remain diagnostic candidates until exact operations and ordinary durable
admission are separately authorized.
