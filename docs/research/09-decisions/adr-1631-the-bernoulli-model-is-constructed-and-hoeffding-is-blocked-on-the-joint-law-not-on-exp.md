# ADR-1631: The Bernoulli model is constructed, and Hoeffding is blocked on the joint law — not on `exp`

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-09-05
Index-summary: `AlgS.OrderedRing.bernoulliVar`/`bernoulliMass` are the first named distribution in the shelf and the first `IsDistribution` discharged from a construction rather than assumed; `E[X] ≃ q` and `Var[X] ≃ q·(1−q)` are proved over the record with NO `mulComm`, because the only commutativity the identity needs is `(1−q)·q ≃ q·(1−q)` and both sides expand to `q + −(q·q)`. The binomial's mean and variance are two lines over the existing `ℚ` shelf, but they live at `ℚ` and not over the record, because the variance of a sum needs `variance_eq`, which needs `mulComm` — a measured record boundary, not a mathematical one. Hoeffding did NOT land, and the blocker is NOT the exponential: `CReal.expFn` exists, `CReal.expFn_add` does not, and before either matters Hoeffding needs `E[∏ f(X_j)] = ∏ E[f(X_j)]`, a statement about a joint law over a product space that this one-weight-function development cannot express at all. The reachable tail bound is Markov at the fourth power, and that landed. Three mutants were RUN; all three were killed by the trusted gate, and that uniformity is itself the finding.
Index-status: accepted

## Context

Roadmap **W3-12**. [ADR-1616](adr-1616-finite-probability-over-algs-orderedring-independence-and-the-integral-bridge.md)
stated the finite-probability layer once over `(R : AlgS.OrderedRing)`:
`sumRange`, `IsDistribution`, `expectation`, `variance`, `covariance`,
`Independent`, Markov, and twenty-odd supporting theorems. The `ℚ` side
(`rat_prelude/probability.rs`, 6,839 lines) additionally has `variance_eq`,
`variance_add_of_uncorrelated`, `sumVars`, `expectation_sumVars`,
`variance_sumVars`, `PairwiseUncorrelated`, Chebyshev, `variance_indicator`
and a Bernoulli law of large numbers.

What neither side had is a **distribution**. Every statement in both files
takes an arbitrary weight function `p`; no `p` is ever built, so no
`IsDistribution` hypothesis is ever discharged from a construction. `ℚ`'s
`Rat.indicator a X` is a threshold indicator on an arbitrary space, and
`Rat.variance_indicator` says its variance is `μ(1 − μ)` where `μ` is
*whatever* its expectation happens to be — a true and useful theorem, but not
"the Bernoulli(q) distribution has mean `q`", because there is no `q`.

`Rat.bernoulli` is Bernoulli's *inequality*.

## Decision

### 1. The Bernoulli model is `Nat.rec` over the record's own field set

`crates/axeyum-lean-kernel/src/rat_prelude/binomial_s.rs`, on the two-outcome
index range `{0, 1}`:

```text
AlgS.OrderedRing.bernoulliVar  R   : Nat → R.carrier
  := fun k => Nat.rec R.zero (fun _ _ => R.one) k
AlgS.OrderedRing.bernoulliMass R q : Nat → R.carrier
  := fun k => Nat.rec (R.add R.one (R.neg q)) (fun _ _ => q) k
```

Both reduce by ι alone at `0` and `1`, so every theorem below states the clean
form and lets the kernel's defeq close the gap to the reduced two-term sum.
The failure weight is written `add one (neg q)` rather than through `AlgS.sub`
**because that is the shape `AlgS.OrderedRing.sub_nonneg_of_le` already
produces** — the nonnegativity proof is then that theorem applied, with no
rewriting.

`bernoulliMass_nonneg` is stated for **every** `k`, not for `k < 2`. That is
what lets `bernoulli_isDistribution` discharge `∀ i, i < 2 → …` with
`fun i _ => …` and never invert a `Nat.lt`. The bounded form would have cost a
two-case-plus-absurd analysis for nothing.

### 2. The variance needs commutativity exactly once, and it is derivable

`AlgS.OrderedRing` carries no `mulComm` (ADR-1592 §2), and
`Var[X] = E[(X − μ)²] ≃ q·(1 − q)` looks like it needs one. It does not: the
subring generated by a single element is commutative, and the *one* instance
the identity uses is

```text
hcomm : (1 − q)·q ≃ q·(1 − q)
```

which is proved by expanding both sides to `q + −(q·q)` — `distribR` with
`mulOneL` and `neg_mul` on the left, `distribL` with `mulOneR` and `mul_neg`
on the right. With `u := 1 − q` and `hcomm` in hand the whole computation is
four steps:

```text
(q·q)·u + (u·u)·q  ≃  q·(q·u) + u·(q·u)        [mulAssoc twice, hcomm]
                   ≃  (q + u)·(q·u)            [distribR, backwards]
                   ≃  1·(q·u)                  [q + u ≃ 1]
                   ≃  q·u
```

`q + u ≃ 1` is `addAssoc`/`addComm`/`negAdd`/`addZero`. No trichotomy, no
field inverse, no commutative ring.

Two generic ring lemmas the spine did not have are declared for this and are
worth having on their own: **`AlgS.OrderedRing.mul_neg`** (`a·(−b) ≃ −(a·b)` —
ADR-1616 proved only the LEFT-handed `neg_mul`, and without `mulComm` the two
are different statements, which `binomial_s_tests.rs` asserts by requiring the
kernel to refuse to identify their types) and **`AlgS.OrderedRing.zero_add`**
(the record's additive unit law is right-handed, and every sum reduced out of
`sumRange` starts with a literal `zero` on the left).

### 3. The binomial's moments are two lines — at `ℚ`, and the boundary is the record

`crates/axeyum-lean-kernel/src/rat_prelude/binomial_rat.rs`:

```text
Rat.binomial_expectation : ∀ X p n m q,
  (∀ j, j < m → E[X j] = q) → E[Σ_{j<m} X_j] = m·q
Rat.binomial_variance : ∀ X p n, IsDistribution p n → ∀ m q,
  PairwiseUncorrelated X m p n →
  (∀ j, j < m → Var[X j] = q(1−q)) → Var[Σ_{j<m} X_j] = m·q(1−q)
Rat.binomial_chebyshev  -- Chebyshev with that variance substituted
```

Each of the first two is `expectation_sumVars` / `variance_sumVars` followed
by **one shared helper** (`sumRange_congr_lt` against the constant function,
then `sum_range_const`). The two proofs differ only in which lemma and which
constant they hand the helper. That is the measurement W3-12 asked for: the
shelf built by ADR-1616 and its `ℚ` predecessor really does make these two
lines.

**Why they are at `ℚ`.** `probability_s.rs`'s own header states the
obstruction and this lane confirms it is binding:
`AlgS.OrderedRing.variance` is the centred form and `covariance` the
computational one, and relating them needs `E[fun k => X k · c] ≃ E[X]·c`,
which pulls a constant past the weight `p k` and therefore needs `mulComm`.
So the generic shelf has no `variance_eq`, hence no
`variance_add_of_uncorrelated`, hence no variance of a sum. This is a fact
about the RECORD, not about the mathematics: **the moment
`AlgS.CommOrderedRing` exists, all three theorems move up verbatim.** The mean
alone would generalize today; splitting the pair across two carriers is worse
than stating both where the variance can live.

Note also what "binomial" can mean here at all. This development has one
weight function over one index range, so `Rat.PairwiseUncorrelated` is the
honest, strictly weaker hypothesis the whole `ℚ` concentration section already
uses in place of independence. A binomial variable is `Rat.sumVars X m` plus
pairwise uncorrelatedness plus identical per-trial moments, and that is
exactly what these theorems assume.

### 4. Hoeffding is blocked on the joint law first, and on `exp`'s functional equation second

Hoeffding did **not** land. The brief anticipated `exp` as the blocker. It is
not the first one, and the exponential is not simply absent:

1. **The joint law.** Hoeffding needs `E[∏_j f(X_j)] = ∏_j E[f(X_j)]`, which
   is a statement about a product space. `probability.rs`'s own header says
   this development cannot state independence for that reason, and
   `shape_search --include-constructed` over **4,260** declarations finds no
   product or joint distribution of any spelling. This is a missing
   *construction*, not a missing lemma.
2. **The functional equation.** `CReal.expFn : CReal → CReal` **exists** — so
   "we have no exponential" would have been the wrong report. What does not
   exist, in the same 4,260-declaration index, is `CReal.expFn_add`
   (`e^{x+y} = e^x·e^y`), without which the product step is not even statable.
3. **The carrier gap.** The moment shelf is `ℚ`-valued and `expFn` is
   `CReal`-valued, so even with (1) and (2) a transfer would be needed.
   `AlgS.OrderedRing.expectation_map` (ADR-1616) is that transfer and is
   already proved, which is the one piece of this that is done.

The theorem that would have been proved, for the record:

```text
Rat.hoeffding : ∀ X p n m q t, IsDistribution p n →
  IndependentFamily X m p n →                       -- (1), does not exist
  (∀ j k, j < m → k < n → le zero (X j k) ∧ le (X j k) one) →
  lt zero t →
  le (P[ Σ_{j<m} X_j − m·q ≥ t ]) (exp (neg (mul two (mul t t))) / m)
                                   -- (2), `exp` has no functional equation
```

### 5. What the carrier CAN state is Markov at the fourth power, and it landed

`Rat.fourth_moment_inequality : ∀ a X p n, IsDistribution p n → 0 < a →
a⁴ · E[𝟙[a⁴ ≤ (X − μ)⁴]] ≤ E[(X − μ)⁴]` — i.e.
`P(|X − E X| ≥ a) ≤ E[(X − E X)⁴]/a⁴`, multiplied through so no `Rat.inv`
appears. It is `Rat.markov_constructed` at threshold `a⁴` against the summand
`((X − μ)²)²`, whose nonnegativity is `Rat.sq_nonneg` applied to `(X − μ)²`
rather than to `X − μ`, and whose threshold positivity is `Rat.mul_pos` twice.
No hypothesis beyond Markov's own.

It is stated for an arbitrary `X`, because that is where its content is; the
binomial specialisation is this theorem at `X := Rat.sumVars X m`, exactly as
`Rat.binomial_chebyshev` is `Rat.chebyshev_inequality` there.

What it does **not** give is Hoeffding's rate. Turning a fourth-moment bound
into a better-than-Chebyshev rate needs `E[(Σ − E Σ)⁴] ≤ 3(m σ²)²`, which
needs **4-wise** uncorrelatedness — a hypothesis this development could state
(it is a statement about `covariance`-like quantities, not about a joint law)
but whose proof is a genuine expansion of a fourth power over `m` terms. That
is the next slice, and it is bounded.

## Consequences

- The trusted base is unchanged: all twelve new declarations are axiom-free,
  asserted after `Environment::contains` (an absent name has an empty
  footprint too).
- `q = 1/2` is **not** an adequate instance for a variance test, and the suite
  says why in its own assertions: `q(1 − q)` and the plausible wrong answer
  `q·q` are BOTH `1/4` there. At `q = 1/3` they are `2/9` and `1/9`.
- Eight private helpers in `probability.rs` are now `pub(super)`
  (`is_distribution`, `expectation`, `const_fn`, `variance`, `nat_as_rat`,
  `sum_range_const`, `pairwise_uncorrelated`, `variance_summand`) — a
  visibility-only change, so `binomial_rat.rs` reuses `sum_range_const`'s
  ninety-line induction instead of copying it.

## Mutation evidence

Registered as `bernoulli-binomial-model` in
`scripts/tests/mutation_controls.py`. Three mutants, all **RUN**, all killed —
and the uniformity is the finding, not a shortcoming of the suite. A
`bernoulli_variance` whose right-hand side is `q·q` is not a wrong theorem the
tests must catch: the proof term is written against the ι-reduct of
`variance … 2`, so its inferred type no longer matches its declared one,
`build_rat_prelude` returns `Err`, and the whole registered suite dies at
`prelude()`. The kill counts are therefore the suite size, not one.

The consequence worth stating: for these declarations the **kernel** is the
guard, and the evaluation tests are insurance against a future change that
alters a definition and its proof together — which is exactly the class the
trusted gate cannot see (a `Definition` type-checks whatever it computes).
That is why `bernoulli_mass_evaluates_at_a_half_and_a_third` and
`bernoulli_variance_at_a_third_is_not_q_squared` exist and why they use `1/3`.
