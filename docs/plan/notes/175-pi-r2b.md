## Was `htail` (`cosWideTailAntitone`) the blocker it was sized as?

**Yes**, and the symbolic cross-multiplication the brief warned about is real,
but it did NOT need to be built as raw `Nat.factorial`/`Rat.normalize`
cross-multiplication. The route that worked instead reuses
`CReal.expTermSuccScale` (`∀ m, Equiv (mul (ofNat (succ m)) (expTerm (succ
m))) (expTerm m)`, landed by an earlier π lane for the derivative work) TWICE
to turn `64·m! ≤ 25·(m+2)!` into a pure `CReal`-order argument:

- `m := idx1 := add (succ k) (succ k) >= 2` always (`2k+2`).
- `expTermSuccScale` at `sm := succ m` and at `m` gives, chained through
  `of_nat_mul`/`mul_assoc`/`mul_comm`: `mul (ofNat K) (expTerm (succ (succ
  m))) ~ expTerm m` EXACTLY, `K := (m+1)*(m+2)`.
- `R² <= 3 <= m+1 <= K` (Nat side: `m>=2` gives `m+1>=3`; `m+2>=1` trivially;
  `Nat.mul_le_mul_left` chains them). `R² <= 3` is a SMALL concrete numeric
  fact (`64/25 <= 3`), closed not by hand-rolled cross-multiplication but by
  `Rat.ble`'s own COMPUTATION: `Rat.ble (8/5 * 8/5) (3/1)` reduces to
  `Bool.true` by iota alone on these small literals, so
  `Rat.le_of_ble_eq_true` needs only `Eq.refl` at `Bool.true` — no
  `Rat.normalize_cross` battery at all. This is a cheaper, more general
  technique than the brief's proposed route and is worth reusing anywhere a
  SMALL concrete `Rat.le` fact is needed.
- `mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_left` (twice — once to scale by `R²<=K`, once to
  scale the result by `pow R m >= 0`) plus a three-factor commute helper
  (`mul_left_comm_c`, the multiplicative mirror of this file's own
  `add_right_comm_c`) close the rest.
- The Nat-side "`idx1 >= 2`" fact needed one non-trivial step:
  `add (succ k) (succ k)` peels ONE `succ` by iota (right-argument
  recursion), but the SECOND `succ` sits on the LEFT and needs
  `Nat.add_comm` to reach — matching the pattern this file's own
  `dbl_succ_bridge`/`cos_magnitude_dec` (trig.rs) already use for the
  sibling index identity.

Total new code: ~340 lines (`cos_sign.rs`), roughly the size the brief
implied ("~130 lines" for one inequality, times two applications of
`expTermSuccScale` plus the Nat-side bookkeeping and the numeric bound).

## Items 3-4: NOT built, and the reason is a genuine new gap, not laziness

`alternatingUpperBoundTail`'s remaining hypothesis is
`Converges (sumRange t) L` for `t k := mul (pow (neg one) k) (a k)`,
`a := cosWideTail*`'s magnitude sequence. The natural source is
`CReal.cosFnWideUniformConverges : UniformConvergesOn cosFnTerm cosFnWide
zero R`, whose `.spec` at a fixed point `x` gives a `close_within`-shaped
fact: `le (abs (add (F n x) (neg (G x)))) (ofRat (natDivSucc rate n))`.

**`Converges`'s own definition is different in kind**: `∃ K, ∀ n, Within (seq
(f n) n − seq L n) (natDivSucc K n)` — a bound on the RATIONAL representative
sequences at a SHARED index `n`, not a `CReal`-level `le`/`abs` bound.
Bridging `close_within` down to `Within` needs, at minimum:

1. Split `close_within` into two one-sided `le`s (`le_abs_self`/`neg_le_abs`
   + `le_trans`) — cheap.
2. `CReal.within_of_two_sided_le` (the ONE general "real inequality -> Within
   at a chosen index" bridge that exists anywhere in this tree — confirmed by
   reading `order_extra.rs` and grepping every `close_within`/`Within` site in
   `creal/`) turns those into `Within (seq D i) (seq y i + 2/(i+1))` for a
   DIFFERENCE term `D`.
3. `D`'s sequence is `seq (add (F n x) (neg (G x))) i`, and `CReal.add` has
   Bishop's OWN index shift (`(x+y)_n := x_{2n+1}+y_{2n+1}`) — so this is
   NOT yet `seq (F n x) i − seq (G x) i` at a shared `i`; it needs a
   `CReal.regular`-based bridge across the shift, the same kind of
   construction `CReal.converges_add`'s own doc names as "the previous
   slice's blocker" and its own non-trivial rate accounting.

So this is not "mechanical given `htail`" as `174-pi-rung2.md` characterised
it — it is a THIRD general bridge, comparable in size to `converges_add`
itself, that does not exist anywhere in this tree today. Confirmed by
reading (not guessing): `creal/convergence.rs`, `creal/order_extra.rs`, every
`close_within`-mentioning file (`crossing.rs`, `exp_fn.rs`, `derivative.rs`,
`ivt.rs`, `integral.rs`, `uniform_convergence.rs`, `uniform_continuity.rs`).

**Item 4** (the numeric `sumRange t 3 <= 0` evaluation) was not attempted
since it has no consumer without item 3, but the `Rat.ble`-computation
technique this lane used for `R²<=3` likely also closes item 4's numeric
leaf cheaply, inside the stated 60,000 `Nat.mul` budget — worth trying first
before falling back to `on_a_deep_stack` + `normalize`-chain arithmetic.
