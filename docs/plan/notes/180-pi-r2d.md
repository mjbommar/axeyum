**The literal "rescale every term to a Rat value with denominator 1875"
reading of the brief is impossible in this kernel and was abandoned.** `Rat`
carries an intrinsic `reduced` field (`gcd(|num|,den)=1`); `1875/1875`
(gcd=1875) and `-2400/1875` (gcd=75) are not reduced, so no `Rat.mk` proof
term exists for them — the type itself refuses an un-reduced representative.
What's implemented instead: each term collapses to its OWN naturally-reduced
flat value (`1/1`, `-32/25`, `512/1875`), not a shared-denominator one; the
"combine over 1875" arithmetic happens implicitly inside `Rat.add`'s own
cross-multiplication on those flat values, at a bounded ~46,875 scale rather
than the literal per-term rescale the brief's prose suggested.
