## QF_DT — +78, and 86% of it is one function

Diagnosed 2026-09-12 by lane WHY-UNKNOWN once reason coverage reached 100%.

**70 of the 81 gap files give one sentence from one function**
(`datatype_native.rs:432`, `expect_dt_symbol`): *"`is`/`select` over a
non-variable datatype term (constructors should fold first)"*. Classifying that
operand: a datatype-sorted `ite` (28 files), a constructor application (43), a
nested selector (17), 13 unresolved because the scan does not substitute `let`.

**Three folds, not a decision procedure.** The remaining 11 files are a genuine
incompleteness in the traversed-field relaxation — separate work.

**A second defect, same division**: on **73 of 81** the canonicalizer refuses a
datatype-sorted term outright (`sort mismatch: expected Bool or BitVec, found
(Datatype n)`), so every one silently loses its entire preprocessing pass.

|---|---:|---:|

**The width remedy is measured and does not pay** ([ADR-1921](../research/09-decisions/adr-1921-the-int-blast-width-escalation-is-measured-and-not-shipped.md)).
An escalating ladder (32 → 40/48/56/64) decides **0 of 110** at the competition
budget — 0 gains, 0 losses, 0 disagreements, +4.6% wall — and turns 23 precise
overflow diagnoses into uninformative `Timeout`/`Watchdog`. It is not a clock
problem either: at 150 s the escalation decides 3 of the 23 width-family files
and the **baseline at the same 150 s decides the same three with the same
verdicts**, so the escalation contributed zero; 14 of the other 20 overflow at
**width 64**, which is the blaster's hard ceiling (`MAX_INT_BLAST_WIDTH`, gated
by the `i128` model read-back — 128 is unreachable without a bigint read-back).
`AXEYUM_INT_BLAST_ESCALATION_MAX_WIDTH` now exists so the next population can be
measured without a rebuild; it ships as a no-op.

**Where a QF_NIA lane should go: the dispatch timeout (41).** The CNF clause
budget (30) already carries a measured negative — lifting it by the estimator's
own 9.4x slack decides 0 of 49 (`notes/118-nia-diagnosis.md`). One unmeasured
hypothesis: `blast_integers` emits a no-overflow constraint for `int_mul` and
for nothing else, so the surviving replay failures are ADDITIVE wraparound.

## Not dispatched, deliberately

**The 55 timeouts**, concentrated in QF_LRA (22) and QF_NRA (10). The headroom
probe settled that these are not near the boundary: **0 of 40 QF_LRA gap files
decide even at a 120 s budget**. More time does not reach them, so the route is
wrong for these problems rather than slow. That is a design decision about real
arithmetic, not a lane.

**The 10 wide-integer literals** are the ADR-1702 population: 26 QF_UFLIA files
carrying 78-digit 2^256 EVM words. ADR-0376's ablation closed it — strip every
wide literal and 6 of 6 are still `unknown`, so the binding constraint is the
decision procedure, not the literal type.

### The watchdog is real, and it is bigger than the first census said

The first census sampled 24 files per division and put the watchdog at 57. The
full board at the strict threshold — we return `unknown`, a reference decides,
and wall time is at or past `timeout_ms + 1 s`, which is the harness killing a
worker that did not return — is **149**:

| division | watchdog | division | watchdog |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| QF_UFLRA | **51** | QF_RDL | 6 |
| QF_LIA | 28 | UF | 6 |
| QF_IDL | 19 | QF_S | 3 |
| QF_UFLIA | 14 | QF_BV | 2 |
| QF_NIA | 7 | QF_SLIA | 2 |
| QF_LRA | 6 | QF_NRA | 5 |

Do not confuse this with the 305 files whose wall time is merely at or past
23 s. Most of those are clean timeouts — `unknown` returned **at** the budget,
which is correct behaviour. Only the kill is a defect.

**Two defects, both the same shape: a phase the budget cannot see.**

1. `dpll_t::Abstractor::abstract_term` was an unmemoised *tree* walk over a
   **DAG**, exponential in the sharing, and consulted no clock at all.
2. `simplex::feasible` passed the literal `None` as its deadline, so the whole
   one-shot `lra::simplex_fallback` route was unbounded — while the
   `Incremental` engine passed a real one. Three further stretches of
   `lra::decide_within` took no deadline either.

**Measured gain, FINAL. Interleaved per file, both arms on one core**:
QF_UFLIA **+5**, QF_UFLRA **+2**, QF_LRA **+0**, others **+0**. Zero losses and
zero flips across ~110 files. Both converted files agree with z3, cvc5 and their
declared `:status`. Merged `47f902ea1`; data in
`bench-results/deadline-overrun-20260912/`.

**The 51 is not headroom.** All 51 QF_UFLRA watchdog files were run, not
sampled. **2 convert.** The other 49 are two families
(`cpachecker-induction.32_1_cilled…` and `…minepump_spec*_product*`) that still
land at 25.02–25.04 s, unchanged, while z3 decides them in 0.2–0.5 s. A census
of a failure MODE is not a count of fixable files — that is now three times in
one session, so treat every count in this file the same way until someone runs
the whole population.

**One apparent loss was a load flake**, proven by three interleaved re-runs on a
quiet core: the file sits on the 24 s boundary and flips for BOTH arms.
Interleaving removes the systematic component of ambient load, not its variance.

**The residual is not where the lane predicted.** Its note names `lra::solve`
(Fourier–Motzkin) "on a path whose deadline is still `None`". On merged main
`lra::solve` takes a deadline, polls it in both loops, and `lra::decide_within`
passes a real one. The real obstacle is that **a watchdog kill destroys its own
diagnosis**: a killed `minepump` run records five routes through 38 ms and then
loses 25 s of evidence, so the trail's last entry names a route that had already
returned. Lane `WATCHDOG-RESIDUAL` (ADR-1926) starts with the instrument.

**Named as still open by the lane, not hidden**: the
`cpachecker-induction.32_1_cilled…` family still reaches the watchdog. The
residual is `lra::solve` (Fourier–Motzkin) on a path whose deadline is still
`None` above it.

### QF_NIA is not a width problem — the first census was wrong

The row above says "half known" on the strength of a 14-file sample. Over all
**110** winnable files the width family is **26 (24%)**, behind the preprocessed
dispatch timeout (41) and the CNF clause budget (30). An interleaved A/B of the
escalation over 220 solves gives **0 gains, 0 losses, +4.6% wall**, and it
replaces 23 precise `overflowed at width 32` diagnoses with uninformative
timeouts. At a 150 s budget the escalation decides 3 of 23 — and the baseline at
the same 150 s decides the same three files with the same three verdicts, so the
escalation contributed nothing. **14 of the other 20 overflow at width 64**, the
blaster's hard ceiling; 128 is unreachable without replacing the `i128` model
read-back. Shipped: the lever only, byte-identical by construction (ADR-1921).

A QF_NIA lane should target the **preprocessed dispatch timeout** (41 of 110).
The CNF clause budget already has a measured negative: lifting it by the
estimator's own 9.4x slack decides 0 of 49.

**This is the second time a 14-to-24-file sample has pointed a lane at the wrong
cause.** Both times the file lists were path-sorted, so one family dominated the
head. Census the whole division before briefing.

## Rules for working this log

- **A cause is a census, not a theory.** Every row above cites the measurement.
- **Quote the reference.** The gap is against the BETTER of z3/cvc5 per
  division; the board's own reference differs per division by SMT-COMP standing.
- **Both references run plain.** SMT-COMP gives cvc5 a portfolio
  (`--finite-model-find` among others) that wins exactly the UF files our FMF
  work wins. Closing a gap against a plain reference is not closing it against
  the competition configuration.
- **200 files is a sample** of divisions averaging 17,891. A division's gap here
  is an estimate with a real error bar.
