## QF_DT — +78, and 86% of it is one function

Diagnosed 2026-09-12 by lane WHY-UNKNOWN once reason coverage reached 100%.

**70 of the 81 gap files give one sentence from one function**
(`datatype_native.rs:432`, `expect_dt_symbol`): *"`is`/`select` over a
non-variable datatype term (constructors should fold first)"*. Classifying that
operand: a datatype-sorted `ite` (28 files), a constructor application (43), a
nested selector (17), 13 unresolved because the scan does not substitute `let`.

**Three folds, not a decision procedure.** The remaining 11 files are a genuine
incompleteness in the traversed-field relaxation — separate work.

**A second defect, same division**: on **73 of 81** the canonicalizer refuses a
datatype-sorted term outright (`sort mismatch: expected Bool or BitVec, found
(Datatype n)`), so every one silently loses its entire preprocessing pass.

## QF_NIA — +103, and the width is NOT the lead

**Superseded 2026-09-12 by lane `qf-nia-width`, which classified all 110 rather
than 14.** The row below is what the 14-file sample said; the table under it is
the full population, and the two disagree by a factor of two on the width class.

Sample of 14: overflow-at-width-32 **7**, dispatch timeout 5, CNF clause budget
1, watchdog 1 — read as "half the sample is one cap".

All **110** winnable files
([measurement](../research/03-measurements/qf-nia-is-not-a-width-problem-2026-09-12.md)):

| reason | files | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| preprocessed dispatch timeout after reduced solve | 41 | 37% |
| estimated CNF clauses exceeds budget | 30 | 27% |
| `bounded integer model overflowed at width 32` | 23 | 21% |
| watchdog fired before the worker thread returned | 12 | 11% |
| integer constant does not fit the bounded width 32 | 2 | 2% |
| `distinct` pairwise-expansion limit | 1 | 1% |
| no model within the bounded integer width 32 | 1 | 1% |

**The width remedy is measured and does not pay** ([ADR-1921](../research/09-decisions/adr-1921-the-int-blast-width-escalation-is-measured-and-not-shipped.md)).
An escalating ladder (32 → 40/48/56/64) decides **0 of 110** at the competition
budget — 0 gains, 0 losses, 0 disagreements, +4.6% wall — and turns 23 precise
overflow diagnoses into uninformative `Timeout`/`Watchdog`. It is not a clock
problem either: at 150 s the escalation decides 3 of the 23 width-family files
and the **baseline at the same 150 s decides the same three with the same
verdicts**, so the escalation contributed zero; 14 of the other 20 overflow at
**width 64**, which is the blaster's hard ceiling (`MAX_INT_BLAST_WIDTH`, gated
by the `i128` model read-back — 128 is unreachable without a bigint read-back).
`AXEYUM_INT_BLAST_ESCALATION_MAX_WIDTH` now exists so the next population can be
measured without a rebuild; it ships as a no-op.

**Where a QF_NIA lane should go: the dispatch timeout (41).** The CNF clause
budget (30) already carries a measured negative — lifting it by the estimator's
own 9.4x slack decides 0 of 49 (`notes/118-nia-diagnosis.md`). One unmeasured
hypothesis: `blast_integers` emits a no-overflow constraint for `int_mul` and
for nothing else, so the surviving replay failures are ADDITIVE wraparound.

## QF_LRA — +59, and it is NOT the clock

## QF_UFLIA — +24, and it mostly IS the clock

8 of 13 decide at 120 s. Three decide in **under 24 s of wall clock** yet fail
at a 24 s budget, because `UF_ARITH_LADDER_RESERVE_SHARE` gives the deciding
route only 3/4 of the budget. Measured: `terminal` policy decides
`hash_uns_05_20` at 24 s where the shipped `probe` does not — but `terminal`
costs the +9 files its removal gained, so this is a trade, not a free win.

## Rules for working this log

- **A cause is a census, not a theory.** Every row above cites the measurement.
- **Quote the reference.** The gap is against the BETTER of z3/cvc5 per
  division; the board's own reference differs per division by SMT-COMP standing.
- **Both references run plain.** SMT-COMP gives cvc5 a portfolio
  (`--finite-model-find` among others) that wins exactly the UF files our FMF
  work wins. Closing a gap against a plain reference is not closing it against
  the competition configuration.
- **200 files is a sample** of divisions averaging 17,891. A division's gap here
  is an estimate with a real error bar.
