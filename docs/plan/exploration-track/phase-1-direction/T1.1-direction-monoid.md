## What

Add `crates/axeyum-solver/src/direction.rs` with the four consts, a private-field `Direction`, and a
`const fn compose` implementing intersection.

## Files touched

- new `crates/axeyum-solver/src/direction.rs`
- `crates/axeyum-solver/src/lib.rs`

## Exit criteria

- Fields private; the only constructible values are the four consts and `compose` outputs
- Property tests for commutativity, associativity, idempotence, identity (`EQUIVALENCE`), and
  absorption (`HEURISTIC`)
- `just check` green

## Notes and traps

`#[must_use]` on `compose`. The unforgeability is the whole point: nothing outside the module may
mint a `Direction`, so a chain can never claim a direction stronger than its weakest edge.
