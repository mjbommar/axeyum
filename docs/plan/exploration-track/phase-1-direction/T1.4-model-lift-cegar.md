## What

Separate the model-lift axis from direction, and model CEGAR as a higher-order constructor rather
than an edge: `cegar(over_approx_edge, refiner)` derives `EQUIVALENCE` on convergence and degrades to
`OVER_APPROX` when the round budget ends first, recording which happened.

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-solver/src/bridge.rs`
- `lazy_bv.rs` adapter
- the `abv` lazy-ROW adapter (`auto.rs:3440`)

## Exit criteria

- `cegar(...)` yields the right direction in both outcomes, with provenance recording which
- Replay-against-source enforced in exactly one place

## Notes and traps

Modelling CEGAR as a combinator lets a random search *build* sound refinement loops it was never
hand-given -- that is the actual payoff, not just tidiness.

Open question for the ADR (B6): who records the equivalence-to-over-approx downgrade, and may a
downgraded chain keep its partial refinement state?
