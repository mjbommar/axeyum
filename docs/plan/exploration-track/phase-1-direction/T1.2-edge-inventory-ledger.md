## Notes and traps

Two prose ambiguities to resolve and record, not paper over:

1. `ArrayElim`/`Ackermann` -- the doc says "sound relaxation" (one-sided) for a classically two-sided
   reduction. The *witnessed* direction and the *true* direction differ, and the ledger records neither.
2. `XorGaussian` -- unsat-only, but the direction is never stated in the prose.

Because direction is per-instantiation, this table documents *routes with guards*, not fixed labels.
Say so in the generated header.
