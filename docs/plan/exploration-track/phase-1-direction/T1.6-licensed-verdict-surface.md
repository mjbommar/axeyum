## Notes and traps

Budget-unknown laundering is a real vector: an `UnknownKind::NodeBudget` at a leaf, retried through a
weaker chain that then claims a verdict from stale partial state. Refusals and budget declines must
not share state with subsequent attempts except through the source query.

Incremental scope: an edge applied under `push`/`assume` has a direction valid only for that scope.
MVP -- chains are per-scope and non-reusable.
