## Notes and traps

A1 hinges on whether the search alphabet will ever contain negation/duality edges (validity via
unsat of not-phi, interpolation, abduction sub-queries). Those break verdict-preserving composition
entirely. MVP recommendation: make them **unrepresentable** as `Bridge` and record the restriction
explicitly, so a future lane cannot add one by accident.

Also settle whether `Assurance` (`capabilities.rs:25-40`) and `Direction` stay orthogonal axes. The
phrase "checkable certificate for the hard direction" at `capabilities.rs:28` suggests the ledger
already wants a merge; the review recommends keeping them separate.
