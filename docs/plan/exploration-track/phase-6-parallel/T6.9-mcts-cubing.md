## What

AlphaMapleSAT-style MCTS cubing with a propagation-rate deductive reward in PUCT.

## Files touched

- splitter; the bridge-search crate once it exists

## Exit criteria

- Beats the greedy cutoff on at least one hard family
- Deterministic under a fixed seed

## Notes and traps

AlphaMapleSAT reports up to 27x sequential and 2.3x parallel speedup over march on Kochen-Specker and
Ramsey instances -- direct evidence that a learned route beats hand-tuned lookahead, and it suits this
codebase far better than replicating march.

See deferred ADR C4 before building a second search engine: phase 3 and phase 6 should share one
skeleton.
