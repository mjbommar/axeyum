## What

End-to-end run on a known result in the reachable band (e.g. Schur(4)=44 or a small van der Waerden
number), producing a composed certificate checked entirely by axeyum's own tools.

## Files touched

- scenario and bench only

## Exit criteria

- The composed certificate checks with axeyum-only tooling
- CPU-hours and proof-byte ledger published in STATUS.md

## Notes and traps

**G3 in practice.** The reachable band on one workstation is 10^2-10^4 CPU-hours: Keller was ~202
CPU-h, Hadwiger-Nelson verification ~1 s, EDP C=2 hours on a workstation. PTN-class (~5x10^4 including
verification) is 2-4 months of a dedicated 32-core box.

Disk binds before CPU. Per-cube check-then-delete is mandatory; budget proof-bytes per cube.
