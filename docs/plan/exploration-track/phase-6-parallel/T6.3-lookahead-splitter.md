## What

Lookahead over the existing two-watched-literal propagation in `proof_sat.rs`; variable ranking by
failed-literal/propagation count; a deterministic cutoff (binary-clause-count threshold, tunable).
Emits the cube manifest **and** the cube-tree tautology certificate.

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-cnf/src/cube.rs`

## Exit criteria

- On corpus CNFs, produces N cubes whose exhaustiveness DRAT passes `check_drat`
- Deterministic across runs
- **Soundness-negative test: a dropped cube is rejected**

## Notes and traps

**The tautology certificate is the soundness keystone of the whole phase.** The split is a binary
tree, so exhaustiveness has a linear-size DRAT proof checkable on a small input -- but it must be
*checked*, never trusted from the splitter.

The negative generator must drop, duplicate, and mutate cubes. A splitter that silently loses a branch
yields per-cube proofs that all check while the composed claim is false -- wrong-unsat with every
individual artifact valid.

Split quality is a research artifact: march-class heuristics embody years of tuning, and Schur 5 needed
a hardness predictor because conquer was 78% of its compute. Budget accordingly.
