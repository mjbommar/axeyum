## What

File-backed, watch-indexed RUP with backward checking, plus per-cube LRAT elaboration while proofs
are still small.

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-cnf/src/drat.rs`, `lrat.rs`

## Exit criteria

- Checks a >1 GB synthetic proof in bounded RSS
- Agrees with the existing `check_drat` on the corpus

## Notes and traps

**This is the blocking local fact for the whole phase.** `check_drat` (`drat.rs:58`) is fully
in-memory (`&[DratStep]`), with the active clause set as `Vec<Vec<CnfLit>>`, deletions by linear
`position_of` scan, no watch indices, no backward checking -- roughly quadratic and RAM-bound. It cannot
check one hard cube's proof.

Schur 5's warning is stark: DRAT-to-LRAT conversion (20.5 CPU-yr) plus ACL2 checking (15.6 CPU-yr)
cost **more than the solving** (14 CPU-yr). Prefer engines emitting LRAT natively; `elaborate_drat_to_lrat`
(`lrat.rs:451`) already exists for the native path.
