## What

Predict per-cube hardness and re-split adaptively (the Schur-5 pattern, which produced 65 billion
subproblems from 10.3 million top-level cubes).

## Files touched

- splitter plus orchestrator

## Exit criteria

- Measured PAR-2 improvement over a uniform cutoff on a hard slice (e.g. the ~9 search-bound QF_BV
  instances recorded at PLAN.md:8381)

## Notes and traps

Split/conquer balance is instance-dependent: conquer was 78% of Schur 5's compute versus 38% of PTN's.
A fixed cutoff cannot be right for both.
