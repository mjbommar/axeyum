## What

Rayon pool plus ADR-0235-style per-cube subprocess isolation, a budget ledger, order-insensitive
aggregation, and re-split on budget exhaustion.

## Files touched

- new module in `crates/axeyum-bench` (per ADR-0001, no new crate until the boundary is proven)

## Exit criteria

- **Verdict and certificate identical across `--jobs 1`, `8`, and `32`** on a seed set
- A timed-out cube re-splits; it never reports a verdict

## Notes and traps

Tier-1 verdict determinism is unconditional and is a genuine differentiator over clause-sharing
portfolios: cubes are independent, conflict budgets make per-cube verdicts pure functions, and
aggregation is order-insensitive.

Any wall-clock check *inside* a worker breaks this. Wall clock lives only at the outer kill boundary,
where a timeout is a counted non-certification.

The degenerate case (N engines, 1 cube) is a plain portfolio and can land before the splitter -- that
is the near-term dispatch win.
