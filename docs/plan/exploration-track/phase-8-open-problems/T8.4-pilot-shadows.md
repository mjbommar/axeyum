## What

Derive and encode 10 shadows, starting from the 45 files that already use `decide` in
`@[category test]` statements -- the maintainers' own seed set.

## Files touched

- `artifacts/open-problems/*/`
- `scripts/validate-foundational-example-pack.py` (extend for `shadow.json`)

## Exit criteria

- 10 packs pass the validator
- **Each has an explicit `direction` field**
- >= 5 have a `checked` route (DRAT or CAS); the rest `replay-only`
- **A deliberate wrong-shadow negative fixture is rejected**

## Notes and traps

Shadow derivation is a **new untrusted layer** -- the exact analogue of the encoding-trust gap that
forced the empty-hexagon team to Lean-verify their encoding. A wrong shadow yields a
confidently-labelled artifact about the wrong statement.

The `direction` field is soundness-critical: a shadow counterexample refutes the conjecture only when
the Lean statement's polarity makes it so, and a completed sweep is *never* evidence of truth.
