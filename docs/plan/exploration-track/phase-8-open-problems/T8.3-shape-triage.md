## What

Classify every open row into buckets A-F with a confidence, and publish the bucket fractions.

## Files touched

- `scripts/`
- `docs/foundational-resources/generated/open-problems-triage.md` (via `gen-foundational-dashboards.py`)

## Exit criteria

- Every open row has a bucket and confidence
- A candidate pool (A union B) of 80-150 listed
- **Bucket fractions published as the experiment's first measured result**

## Notes and traps

The C bucket is reliably detected by `Tendsto|limsup|=o\[|atTop|Density` and is 45-50% of the corpus.
61% of files touch R.

The curriculum's `decidable|computable|bounded|undecidable` vocabulary is too coarse to be the
classifier (23 nodes, distribution 1/6/16/0, `undecidable` never used). It becomes the *display*
vocabulary; the A-F taxonomy is the instrument.
