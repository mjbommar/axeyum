## What

Run the pipeline over the full A-union-B pool across all ~316 open Erdos files and publish the census:
per-bucket counts, shadows derived, bounds swept, unknowns.

## Files touched

- packs, registry, dashboards

## Exit criteria

- **The census is the headline deliverable**: what fraction of open Erdos statements admits a bounded
  shadow

## Notes and traps

**The census, not a threshold record, is the deliverable.** Axeyum's measured position (8/113 on the
p4dfa QF_BV slice at 20 s, at parity with Z3 but hard-capped) caps ambition at the hours band; records
cost CPU-years and bespoke encodings.

Registry drift: formal-conjectures flips solved problems in place, so re-harvest per tag and retire
flipped rows or the census will "sweep" already-solved problems.
