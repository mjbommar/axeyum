| | |

| Depends on | T2.4 |

## What

Generate graduated variant ladders from pack `expected.json` `data` fields (scale modulus, width,
dimension), tagged with the parent pack id.

## Files touched

- new generator keyed off pack metadata
- `scripts/validate-foundational-example-pack.py` (validator hook)

## Exit criteria

- At least 20 packs yield a variant ladder whose ground truth is re-established by the same
  validation family
- Every variant carries its parent pack id for split hygiene

## Notes and traps

**Leakage risk is highest here.** Variants share a template with their parent; an instance-level split
will overstate policy quality massively. The parent-id tag is what makes group k-fold possible.

Gated on ADR-queue item in phase 2's list: do variants live under `artifacts/examples/math/` with a
variant schema (touching the 41,696-line validator) or in a separate generated tier?
