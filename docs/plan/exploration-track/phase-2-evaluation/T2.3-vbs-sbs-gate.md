## Notes and traps

**This is the track's first real decision point.** If the ratio is below roughly 1.1 on decidable
instances, axeyum's routes rarely differ in cost and there is nothing for a policy to learn -- phase 3
should be re-scoped or dropped, and the effort redirected to phases 5, 6, and 8, whose payoff does not
depend on route-selection signal.

Measure before building. Do not start T3.4 (the expensive one) on faith.
