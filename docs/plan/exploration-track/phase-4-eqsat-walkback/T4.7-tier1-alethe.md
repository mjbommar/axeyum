## What

Emit Alethe with each rule instance as a premise, validated by an extended checker rule modelled on
`forall_inst_guarded` (re-derive the substitution structurally inside the checker).

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-cnf` (checker)
- `crates/axeyum-solver` (emitter)
- new or extended `Evidence` variant

## Exit criteria

- Emitted proofs pass the extended `check_alethe`
- Carcara compatibility decision recorded
- Soundness-negative: a wrong substitution is rejected by the checker

## Notes and traps

Gated on G2. If chains blow up, implement FMCAD-2022 greedy minimization first or cap at Tier 0.

Z3's coarse `rewrite` proof steps are the cautionary tale: unreplayable macro-steps are what made Z3
reconstruction painful. Axeyum's per-rule-id fine-grained steps deliberately avoid this -- keep it
that way.
