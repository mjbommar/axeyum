## What

Per-class bottom-up cost DP with deterministic tie-breaking, term reconstruction to a `TermId`, and
Tier-0 walk-back: procedural re-application of every chain rule instance plus `check_congruence`
re-validation. Then measure chain length and justification-retention overhead (**G2**).

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-solver/src/eqsat.rs`
- the bridge module
- a `crates/axeyum-bench` example for the measurement

## Exit criteria

- Extracted term differs from the input on seeded examples
- Every chain validated
- **Soundness-negative test: a corrupted ledger entry is rejected**
- G2 numbers recorded; go/no-go on Tier 1 written down

## Notes and traps

**Cost must be the lowered size, not term size.** The repo's own 062-bench data
(`reduction_shrinks_encoding`, `auto.rs:1519-1550`) shows the term DAG shrinking 1375 to 1215 while
the AIG grew 46%. Term-level cost is anti-correlated with downstream cost.

`check_congruence` does **not** validate rule instances (`lib.rs:1298-1300`) -- a wrong rule instance
used as a premise passes. Tier-0's procedural re-application is therefore load-bearing for soundness,
and the negative test must corrupt a **rule instance**, not just the chain.

It is also O(n^2 x iterations), so validate over the chain's node subset, not the whole graph.
