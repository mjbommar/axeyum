## What

Accept a nanoda-style semver range instead of the exact-string format match (`lib.rs:52`); raise
`max_records` (default 2,000,000, below mathlib stream size); verify `ExprId` `u32` headroom; audit the
whnf cache key.

## Files touched

- the importer's `FORMAT_VERSION` handling, `ImportLimits`

## Exit criteria

- A 4.32 fixture imports
- Limits documented for mathlib-scale streams

## Notes and traps

The whnf cache is keyed by `(ExprId, env-size)` (`lib.rs:234-237`), which **strands entries every
declaration** -- a real performance issue at mathlib scale, not just a limit.

`ExprId` is `u32` (`expr.rs`/`lib.rs:316`): a hard 4.29-billion-node cap to verify against a mathlib
export.
