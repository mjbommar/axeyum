## What

Track representations through Buchberger so `ideal_membership_certificate` returns cofactors over the
**original generators**, not over the computed basis.

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-cas/src/groebner.rs`

## Exit criteria

- Every basis element re-derives from the generators
- The membership certificate re-multiplies exactly
- Iteration/coefficient caps produce honest `None`

## Notes and traps

Cofactors over the basis are useless to an external checker that only knows the original problem. The
re-derivation to generators is the whole point.
