## What

Decide where CAS certificate types and checkers live, given that `axeyum-solver` must not depend on
a 47k-line search crate. Options: extract a small shared poly/`mvpoly` kernel; a certificate-only
module in `axeyum-solver`; CAS as out-of-process or dev-dependency search (the polyrith model).
Also specify the determinism, seeding, and resource-limit contract for any bridge *search*.

## Files touched

- `docs/research/09-decisions/` (new ADR)
- `docs/research/08-planning/foundational-dag.md`

## Exit criteria

- ADR accepted; dependency arrows drawn explicitly
- Determinism and seeding contract for CAS-side search recorded

## Notes and traps

**No crate in the workspace currently depends on `axeyum-cas`**, and ADR-0301 deliberately points the
dependency the other way (CAS lowers *to* the solver to certify itself). A careless
`axeyum-solver -> axeyum-cas` edge drags 47k lines of search into the solver's audit surface and
inverts an accepted ADR.

The polyrith cautionary datum belongs in this ADR: its Sage backend was shut down and the tactic
deprecated. Design so the *search* half is replaceable without invalidating already-checked
certificates.
