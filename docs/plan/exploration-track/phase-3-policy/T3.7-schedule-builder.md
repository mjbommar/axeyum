## What

Greedy marginal-PAR-2-gain-per-cap set cover subject to a round budget, with Suda-style
regularization penalizing chains supported by fewer than k instances in a bucket. Emits policy-v1 plus
provenance.

## Files touched

- `crates/axeyum-bench/` or `tools/`
- policy artifact

## Exit criteria

- policy-v1 beats policy-v0 on a held-out split
- VBS gap reported
- Policy build is deterministic from the table (golden regen test)

## Notes and traps

Spider-style discovery overfits small corpora, and Suda (2024) measures exactly this. Regularize,
hold out splits, and let the public slices referee.
