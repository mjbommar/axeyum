## Notes and traps

Quote de Moura and Passmore's *Strategy Challenge in SMT Solving* as the design's lineage: tactics
and tacticals are a **soundness-by-construction** strategy language, so a chain built from sound edges
is sound regardless of how badly the search chose it. Search quality is a completeness and performance
concern only.

Also record honestly that `check_auto_dispatch` already uses `Instant::now` deadlines, so a wall-clock
kill can flip decided-to-unknown across machines. That is pre-existing; the ADR bounds it rather than
pretending it away.
