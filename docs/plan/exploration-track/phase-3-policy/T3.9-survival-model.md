## What

Fit AFT log-normal (or Kaplan-Meier at small n) per (chain, bucket) on the censored sweep table, and
use it where the raw empirical table is thin.

## Files touched

- bench-side, pure offline

## Exit criteria

- Held-out PAR-2 at least as good as the table-only builder
- Censoring handled without mean-imputation

## Notes and traps

Treating timeouts as "slow observations" systematically flatters bad chains. Use survival methods or
leave cells empty -- never impute.
