# Family: SMT, quantified

One division on the board, and the family where our cause knowledge is
weakest.

| Division | Gap | Cause established? |
|---|---:|---|
| [UF](uf.md) | 8 | **no** — both hypotheses refuted or unverified |

UF is the only division on the board where **no lever is authorised**, because
no front-door cause exists. Two hypotheses have been recorded and neither
survives: finite-model finding was never measured, and the census class was an
artifact of running the flat assertion view instead of the shipped front door.
Neither Boolean-search slice moved the division at all.

It is also the division where we are furthest *ahead* in absolute terms: 24
files the reference does not solve, the widest such column on the board.
