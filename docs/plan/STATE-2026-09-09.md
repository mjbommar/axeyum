## The strategic finding, which is verified

`docs/research/03-measurements/budget-curve-2026-09-08.md`, 8,000 scored solver
invocations at 6/12/24/60 s for both solvers:

**At 2.5x the budget we recover 12 of 135 missing files. The other 91% do not
move.** QF_LRA and QF_ABV are flat while the reference climbs — the capability
signature. The remaining gap is mostly procedures we do not have, not slowness.

One trap the lane caught and refused: QF_UFLIA's *ratio* improves at 60 s only
because cvc5 saturates by 6 s. **24 s stays the headline** because it is what
SMT-COMP publishes.

## What landed that is not a file count

- **Timeouts no longer discard instrumentation.** Counters moved out of
  thread-local into a shared board plus a mid-search mirror; a killed run
  reports partial data labelled as partial.
- **The memory limit binds.** It did not before — files reached 26.6 GB against
  an 8 GiB flag. Now 162 MiB on the worst case, 7 files over the limit -> 1.
- **Every UNSAT with inprocessing checks against the ORIGINAL formula** (117 of
  117). Previously the proof was checked against the reduced formula, so one
  link was trusted.
- **A deterministic work budget** (`axeyum-ir::budget`), zero hot-path cost,
  proven identical across processes and under load.
- **Admission-limit registry 114 -> 469 entries**, with a gate that fails when a
  justification's basis disappears. **387 of 469 remain undated (82%)** and that
  number is now printed in the solver's own output.
- **Loss lists re-cut 480 -> 318.** They had been a third stale, and lanes were
  optimising files we already won.
- **~12,000 lines of technique inventory** across Z3 (1,188 entries), cvc5
  (920), Bitwuzla/Kissat/CaDiCaL (408), every citation in the largest one
  machine-verified.

## Things that were tried and refuted, so they are not retried

Blocked clause elimination (measured negative). Failed-literal probing and
ternary resolution (their own authors cut them). A shared simplex across four
divisions (nobody does this). Leading with Gomory cuts (**cvc5 implements none
of its own** — it reads GLPK's pool, behind a flag that defaults off). The
sparse tableau as headline (the pivot's operation count was already sparse).
Binary watches as the clause-database lever (they are 6.8-14.1% of watch
traffic, and the cost is visit *count*). Warm caching priced as a win (1.79x
pre-merge, 1.00x after — the speedup was an arena clone another lane removed).
Raising the quantifier ground ceiling (0 files, and early-returning files then
burn the whole budget). Relevance filtering (0 of 32).
