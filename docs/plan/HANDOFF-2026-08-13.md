## 5. The measured verdict on the CAS bridge

An independent 111-query corpus (`docs/plan/cas-smt-capability-2026-08-12/`)
measured the bridge against a frozen baseline. **Read this before extending
it** — it is deliberately unflattering:

- decided 82 → 87 (**+5**); gaps 16 → 11; **0 alarms, 0 regressions, 0 verdict
  flips**;
- **54 satisfiable queries: none flipped to `unsat`.** An axis of ten queries
  was written specifically to break `cas-int-units` by carrying its exact
  shape while being satisfiable — it declined on every one;
- both **open** problems and all four number-theoretic tripwires still return
  `unknown`, which is the correct conservative answer;
- **`cas-identity-refuter` contributes zero new decisions.** Polynomial
  identity disequalities were already decided in 1–5 ms by `int-real-relax` /
  `nra-real-root`. Its case rests on certificate quality, which that corpus
  does not measure. **The units route is where the real gain is.**

Eleven structural gaps survive a 6× budget, all with one signature:
`nia-linearize` correctly rejects its own relaxation candidate, leaving only
`int-blast-ladder` at integer width 32.
