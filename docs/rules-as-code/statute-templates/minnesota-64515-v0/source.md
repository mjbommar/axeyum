# Source and modeled boundary

Official source: [2025 Minnesota Statutes §645.15](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/645.15),
retrieved 2026-10-11. Normalized section hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).
The edition and retrieval date do not establish a historical effective interval.

For legally prescribed performance periods, the section excludes the initial
day and includes the terminal day, with express cross-reference exceptions.
A terminal Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday is omitted. This template interprets
that omission as advancing to the earliest subsequent admitted day. It models
positive day periods only, conditional on caller-established applicability.

[§645.13](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/645.13) addresses
successive-week notice publication and spacing. Such periods are excluded here.
[§645.14](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/645.14) addresses
calendar-month counting and shorter terminal months. Month periods are excluded.

[§645.44 subd.5](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/645.44#stat.645.44.5)
contains holiday definitions, observance rules and distinctions among government
branches/subdivisions. This template supplies no universal holiday calendar:
callers provide complete holiday facts and their authority within an explicit
coverage window. Example tests use synthetic calendars, not a verified Minnesota
holiday list. Neither successful computation nor solver replay establishes those
calendar facts or legal applicability.

## Source identity revalidation — 2026-10-11

Fresh HTTPS downloads of all four edition-pinned official pages reproduced
every normalized section SHA-256 in the manifest. The comparison starts at
`<div class="section" id="stat.SECTION">`, ends before the history block,
replaces HTML tags with spaces, unescapes HTML entities, collapses whitespace,
and hashes UTF-8 bytes. Thus page navigation and history entries are excluded.
This verifies the recorded source text identity; it does not establish an
effective interval or decide applicability to a particular event.

The official [holiday definition](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/645.44#stat.645.44.5)
contains branch-specific treatment of Indigenous Peoples Day and the Friday
after Thanksgiving. A calendar producer must retain that policy choice and
the statutory exception for a contrary intention rather than presenting one
unqualified Minnesota calendar.
