SO THE MECHANISM IS NOT THE ONE THIS LANE PREDICTED. The lane's `CLEAN` count
(10 of the 81 files in the three datatype buckets) was built as a predictor of
where gains would come from, and the single observed gain came from OUTSIDE
those 81. What the lever did here was make the GROUND SUB-SOLVES INSIDE THE
QUANTIFIER LOOP stronger -- `decide_instantiation`'s `check_auto` on a
quantifier-free query, the same path ADR-2114 §1 traced -- so the loop
converged in 337 ms instead of exhausting 24 s. It is not "the refusal was
removed and the verdict followed".

This is recorded as written rather than folded into the CLEAN framing, because
fitting an observation to the story that preceded it is how a lane manufactures
a false predictor. One mover is one mover: it does not establish the mechanism
for any other file, and the `CLEAN` count should not be quoted as a forecast of
gains until something has measured that it is one.
