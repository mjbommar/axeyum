WHERE A THRESHOLD SEPARATES THE GAIN FROM THE LOSS
==================================================

ADR-2125 section 6.5 named the axis and this is it measured, over the pinned 200
whose sizing that lane committed (`../lra-warm-basis-20260916/sizing.0{0,1}.tsv`).

The three rows whose cold simplex is a HANDFUL OF ENORMOUS SOLVES -- the shape a
warm basis has nothing to reuse across -- are, with their builds:

    builds    ms/build   file
         1    23908.0    latendresse/ecoliFBAyicesTest-3875-4
        28      283.5    latendresse/ecoliMILPglycerolYices3-50000   <- the STABLE-LOSS
        42      548.1    miplib/danoint-266

The rows that are MANY SMALL RE-SOLVES -- the shape the STABLE-GAIN came from --
run 841 to 1,543 builds at 0.4 to 6.4 ms each.

The separation on this population is exact and it is not marginal:

  * every row with `cold_builds >= 51` has `ms/build <= 12.2`;
  * every row with `ms/build > 20` has `cold_builds <= 42`.

So ANY threshold in [43, 51] separates the two shapes on the pinned 200, and a
threshold is being chosen from a window rather than fitted to a point.

THE VALUE REGISTERED IS 64. It is the next power of two above that window:
1.52x the largest "few enormous" row (42 builds) and 13.1x below the smallest
row of the winning family (841). Choosing the window's own edge would make the
threshold a function of this population's largest outlier; 64 is a round number
with margin on both sides, which is what it is registered as.

WHAT 64 COSTS THE ROWS IT ADMITS. `cold_builds / lra_rounds` is 1.00 on every
row above 100 builds, so the screen trips after 64 refinement rounds. On the
winning family that is 64/841 = 7.6 % to 64/1543 = 4.1 % of the file's rounds run
cold before the basis is kept -- the price of not deciding from a clock.

HOW MANY ROWS FALL ON EACH SIDE, pinned 200
    180 reached the lazy-SMT loop, 20 SILENT (they report NOTHING, not zero)
     51 of the 180 are at or above 64 builds  -- the screen ADMITS these
    129 of the 180 are below it               -- the screen REFUSES these, and
                                                 they run exactly as `off` does
That 51 is the CEILING of what the screened arm can move on this population, and
the 129 are the risk it declines to take.

WHAT THIS TABLE CANNOT SAY. ADR-2125's other two movers are NOT in it:
`sc/sc-14.induction.cvc` (the STABLE-GAIN) and `uart/uart-8.induction.cvc` (the
second STABLE-LOSS) are on the HELD-OUT draw, which has no sizing. The pinned
`sc/*` rows are the `.base.cvc` subfamily, not `.induction.cvc`, so reading the
gain's builds off them would be a family-level guess presented as a measurement.
Both are sized separately; see `sizing-heldout.txt`.
