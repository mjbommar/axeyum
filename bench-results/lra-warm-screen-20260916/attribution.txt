READING THIS TABLE
==================

SCOPE, first, because it is narrower than "the -9.2 %".  The delta decomposed
here is `theory_ms` -- the lazy-SMT loop's THEORY LAYER -- over the rows where
the warm decider was BUILT and both arms printed a trail line.  It is not total
wall clock.  A row can save theory time and lose it again in the skeleton, and
the verdict tables are where that nets out.  Both numbers are published because
quoting either alone is a different claim, which is the rule ADR-2125 section
1.3 set for its own two ceilings.

1. THE SAVING IS MOSTLY THE SKIPPED LINEARIZATION, NOT THE BASIS
   --------------------------------------------------------------
   Over 67 rows in the `on` arm, of 303,177 ms of theory-layer saving:

       linearization   226,396 ms   74.7 %
       basis           111,042 ms   36.6 %
       Fourier-Motzkin   1,822 ms    0.6 %
       residual        -36,083 ms  -11.9 %

   ADR-2125 section 8.5 said in as many words that a successor "should not
   attribute the saving to the basis on this evidence".  It should not, and now
   there is a number: **roughly three quarters of it is the `Collector` rebuild
   a cube the decider answers never pays for.**

   The RATIO is not the one the sizing predicted, and that is worth recording.
   ADR-2125 section 1.3 priced the simplex alone at a 4.15 % median share of
   wall clock and simplex-plus-linearization at 24.21 %, which puts the
   linearization at about 4.8x the basis.  Measured here the ratio is
   226,396 : 111,042 = **2.0x**.  The basis is worth relatively MORE than its
   ceiling implied -- a ceiling is an upper bound on one term, not a prediction
   of how two terms divide.

2. THE RESIDUAL IS NEGATIVE AND IS PRINTED RATHER THAN ABSORBED
   --------------------------------------------------------------
   -36,083 ms, -11.9 %.  The three terms over-explain the delta: the warm arm
   spends about 36 s MORE inside `theory_ms` on something none of them names.
   `theory_ms` covers the whole `cube_check`, which includes materializing a
   real model and mapping a Farkas refutation back through `rows_to_core`, and
   neither has a field.  A decomposition that folded this into the basis term
   would have reported the basis at 25 % instead of 37 %, which is why the
   residual is a column.

3. THE FOURIER-MOTZKIN TERM IS 0.6 % POOLED AND 96 % ON ONE FILE
   ---------------------------------------------------------------
   Both statements are true and neither survives alone.

   `QF_LRA/clock_synchro/clocksynchro_2clocks.main_invar.base.smt2`, off arm:

       theory_ms=1905  cube_collect_ms=23  cube_fm_ms=1822  cube_simplex_ms=45

   **The cold path decides that file's cubes by Fourier-Motzkin**, and 1,822 of
   its 1,905 ms of theory time is FM.  The warm decider replaces it outright
   (`theory_ms=7` in the `on` arm), so on this row the lever is not a warm basis
   against a cold one -- it is a DIFFERENT ENGINE against Fourier-Motzkin.  And
   the 1,822 ms is the ENTIRE pooled FM term: one file is 100 % of it.

   ADR-2125 was careful that the WARM side never answers from Fourier-Motzkin --
   a theory with no warm tableau refuses rather than falling back, precisely so
   an A/B of the lever is not an A/B of two engines.  Nothing made the COLD side
   stop using it, and nothing had to; but a per-file saving measured against
   that cold side can be an engine swap, and this is the file where it is.

   It is 0.6 % of the population, so it moves no conclusion here.  It is
   recorded because a successor reading a single file's -1,906 ms would
   otherwise credit it to the basis.

4. THE SCREEN'S HALF-SAVING IS THE MECHANISM, VISIBLE ON ONE ROW
   ---------------------------------------------------------------
   Same file, the screened arm: `theory_ms=939`, `cube_fm_ms=896`,
   `cube_decisions=64`.  The file runs 128 rounds in every arm.  The screened
   arm decides exactly **64** cubes on the cold path -- the threshold -- and the
   remaining 64 on the warm basis, and its theory time is 939 ms against the off
   arm's 1,905: **49.3 %**, where 64/127 = 50.4 %.

   That is the screen doing arithmetic it was told to do, read off a real file
   rather than a fixture.
