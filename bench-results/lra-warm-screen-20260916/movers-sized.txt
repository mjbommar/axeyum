ADR-2132: ADR-2125's THREE MOVERS, EACH WITH ITS BUILDS-PER-FILE COUNT
======================================================================

ADR-2125 section 6.5 published three re-checked movers and offered ONE
explanation for them -- the axis a successor should screen on:

    "it wins where the cubes are many and small and loses where they are
     few and large"

Two of the three were not in its sizing table.  It said so, and labelled the
reading for the second loss a HYPOTHESIS:

    "`uart-8.induction.cvc`, the second loss, is NOT in the sizing table, so the
     builds-per-file reading is offered for it as a HYPOTHESIS and not as a
     measurement: nothing here has counted its builds.  Saying otherwise would
     be fitting the explanation to the two rows that suit it."

Counted here, on `axeyum.v1` (sha256 91675258916e4aeb), s5 core 5, 24 s budget,
`--trace`, the route trail's own `simplex_cold_builds`:

  verdict under     builds   ms/build  atoms  cold_ms  cold_pivots  file
  ADR-2125's A/B
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  STABLE-LOSS           28      283.5      -     7937            -  latendresse/ecoliMILPglycerolYices3-50000
  STABLE-GAIN        1,815        1.7    976     3044      466,693  sc/sc-14.induction.cvc
  STABLE-LOSS        1,598        1.0  1,272     1670      252,412  uart/uart-8.induction.cvc

(The first row is from ADR-2125's own committed sizing; the other two are new.)

THE HYPOTHESIS IS REFUTED, AND THAT IS THIS LANE'S CENTRAL FINDING
------------------------------------------------------------------

`uart-8.induction.cvc` is 1,598 builds at 1.04 ms each.  That is the MANY SMALL
RE-SOLVES shape -- the winning shape -- not the few-enormous one.  It sits on
the same side of every threshold in the window the pinned sizing measured
([43, 51]), and on the same side as the gain at every threshold whatever:
1,598 and 1,815 are 14 % apart.

So a builds-per-file screen CANNOT remove that loss.  It removes the pinned
loss (28 builds, refused at any threshold above 42) and leaves the held-out one
exactly where it was.  Against ADR-2132's ship criterion -- 0 stable losses on
BOTH draws -- the screen was therefore already unable to ship when this was
measured, and it was measured before the threshold was written into the code.

THE THRESHOLD WAS NOT THEN MOVED, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE
---------------------------------------------------------

A threshold of 1,700 separates 1,598 from 1,815 and would "fix" the held-out
loss.  It is not taken.  Those two rows ARE the held-out evaluation population,
and a boundary drawn between two of its members is fitted to the set it is
scored on -- which is the one thing a held-out draw exists to prevent.  The
value stays at 64, chosen from the pinned sizing's window before any of this
was known, and the lane reports what that value does rather than what some
other value would have done.

WHAT SEPARATES THEM INSTEAD -- AN OBSERVATION, NOT A SCREEN
------------------------------------------------------------

Offered as two points and labelled as two points.  The warm arm replaces
(linearize + cold simplex) per cube with (sync + warm solve), and `sync_cube`
is O(live atoms) while the cold simplex is O(pivots).  So the trade should turn
on cold WORK PER ATOM, and on these two rows it does:

    sc-14   (gain)   466,693 pivots / 1,815 builds =  257 pivots per build
                     257 / 976 atoms              = 0.263 pivots per atom
    uart-8  (loss)   252,412 pivots / 1,598 builds =  158 pivots per build
                     158 / 1,272 atoms            = 0.124 pivots per atom

A 2.1x separation, and it is CLOCK-FREE -- pivots and atoms, not milliseconds,
so a screen on it would not break determinism the way a wall-clock screen would.

This is NOT built and NOT registered.  Two points is a fit, not a discriminator,
and both of them are held-out rows: building a screen on them would spend the
blind population that is supposed to score the next one.  It is recorded as the
shape a successor should SIZE first, on the pinned 200, exactly as this lane
sized builds-per-file before touching the code.
