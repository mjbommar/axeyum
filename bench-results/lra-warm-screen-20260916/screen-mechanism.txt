ADR-2132: DOES THE SCREEN DO WHAT IT SAYS, ON REAL ROWS?
=========================================================

Read off the interleaved three-arm A/B's own columns, over the COMPLETE 400 rows
of the two `QF_LRA` draws (200 pinned + 200 held-out), binary `axeyum.v1`
(sha256 91675258916e4aeb). This is a MECHANISM check, not the verdict table;
the verdicts are in `ab-summary.txt`.

    400 rows | 244 below 64 builds | 123 at or above | 33 SILENT

1. BELOW THE THRESHOLD, THE SCREEN IS SHUT.  **0 of 244**
   -------------------------------------------------------
   Not one row with `a_cold_builds < 64` had the screened arm answer a single
   cube on a warm basis. That is the screen's whole contract -- below the
   threshold the file runs the route it runs today -- checked on 244 real
   files rather than on the two the fixture builds.

   And `warm_cube_cold_restarts` is **0 summed over both treatment arms across
   all 400 rows**. That is the tripwire: a warm basis quietly being rebuilt
   gives identical verdicts, identical churn counts, and differs only in the
   clock, so nothing else in this table could see it.

2. ABOVE IT, THE SCREEN OPENS ON 117 OF 123 -- AND THE SIX IT DOES NOT ARE A
   FINDING ABOUT THE SIZING AXIS, NOT ABOUT THE SCREEN
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   All six are `sc/*.induction3.cvc` -- sc-7, sc-11, sc-17, sc-18, sc-24 and
   sc-33, at 70 to 3,308 cold builds. **One subfamily, not a scattering.**
   They render
   `warm_cube_build=off` in the `on` arm TOO, and `on` builds its decider
   unconditionally whenever the linear offline loop is entered -- so that loop
   was never entered.

   READ FROM THE CAPTURE RATHER THAN INFERRED. On `sc-24.induction3`:

       ; lazy-smt reading=not-reached lra_entries=0 nra_entries=0 nia_entries=0
                  atoms=0 simplex_cold_builds=2264 warm_cube_build=off
       ; route decided_by=none bound_by=lira-dpll last=fd:bounded-completeness-unsat

   **No lazy-SMT loop ran at all.** All 2,264 from-scratch tableaux come from the
   `lira-dpll` rung. (An earlier draft of this file said "the NRA loop", reading
   across from ADR-2125 section 6.4's warning about the shared counters. That was
   a guess at the mechanism and the capture refutes it: `nra_entries` is 0 too.)

   **So `simplex_cold_builds` counts every route that calls
   `feasible_within_sparse`, not the offline lazy-SMT loops.** Two consequences,
   and they point opposite ways:

   * THE SCREEN IS UNAFFECTED, and the reason is a design choice that now has a
     measured example behind it. `WarmCubeScreen::Waiting` snapshots the counter
     when the LINEAR LOOP IS ENTERED and compares a DELTA, so a rung that burned
     2,264 builds before the loop started contributes nothing to the comparison.
     Had the screen read the counter absolutely -- the obvious implementation --
     this file would have tripped it on its first round.
   * THE SIZING AXIS OVER-COUNTS on files like these. The threshold was derived
     from the pinned 200, where the error is **exactly zero**: all 68 rows with
     a nonzero build count have `lra_entries > 0`, checked against ADR-2125's own
     committed sizing. Over the complete 400 it is 6 of 123. That is the
     training population failing to contain a shape the evaluation one has, which
     is what a held-out draw is for.

   A successor sizing any screen on this counter should split it per route
   first. This lane did not; 0 of 68 pinned and 6 of 123 over the complete 400
   is how far it can say the error goes.

3. THE SCREEN ANSWERS FEWER CUBES THAN `on` ON 112 OF THE 117 IT OPENS ON
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------
   That is the screen's signature: the rounds below the threshold ran cold in
   one arm and warm in the other. The gap is 78-86 cubes on the smaller files,
   which is the ~64-round screen plus the different rate the two arms then run
   at.

   The five exceptions are `unknown` in all three arms; two of them:

       a_builds=938  on= 886  screened= 914   sc/sc-25.base.cvc
       a_builds=931  on=1102  screened=1109   sc/sc-40.induction2.cvc

   Neither is a defect. At a 24 s wall-clock budget the number of cubes an arm
   answers is a race, not a property: an arm that runs marginally faster reaches
   more rounds inside the budget. The in-process fixture
   `a_file_that_crosses_the_threshold_mid_run_decides_what_off_decides` asserts
   `on > screened` with NO deadline, where it is deterministic, which is why
   that assertion belongs in the fixture and not in this table.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
-----------------------
That the screen is right. It shows the screen is doing what it was built to do
on the population it was aimed at. Whether doing that is worth anything is the
verdict table's question, and section 2 of the ADR already answers half of it:
the held-out stable loss is at 1,598 builds and no threshold in the sizing
window separates it from the gain.
