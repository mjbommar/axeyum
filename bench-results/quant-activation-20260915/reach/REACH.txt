QUANT-ACTIVATION -- DOES THE LEVER REACH ITS OWN TARGET ON THE REAL CORPUS?

The A/B answers "does the lever CONVERT a file". This answers the step before:
is a widened registration produced and HANDED OFF at all. The two disagree in
the way that matters --- a lever that reaches its target and does not convert is
a capability question, while one that never reaches it is a plumbing failure
reported as a null, and a sweep that prints `+0` cannot tell them apart.

POPULATION. 24 of the 174 `widen_target` undecided files in `UFLIA`, `AUFLIRA`
and `UFNIA`, sampled EVENLY SPACED within each division's path-sorted list and
then round-robined ACROSS the three. Not a prefix: a prefix of a path-sorted
corpus list is one directory, not a sample of a division, and reading one has
published false nulls in this repository before.

INSTRUMENT. `reach-probe.sh`, `AXEYUM_QPROBE=1` AND `AXEYUM_QPROBE_CENSUS=1` ---
both, because `AdmissionCensus::enabled` is the AND of the two and with only the
first every `rej_*` field prints 0. Reading those zeros as "nothing was
rejected" attributes a cause to a measurement nobody took (ADR-2113 §6 records
that exact error).

    rej_nocontext   the universal is a registration with NO context: its tuples
                    are joined and then DISCARDED
    rej_handoff     the universal HAS a context: its tuples went to the
                    positive-replacement driver instead

RESULT.

    rows                                24
    probe printed on EITHER arm           2
    probe printed on NEITHER arm         22
    handoff ROSE from arm A to arm B      2  (of the 2 that printed)
    verdict changed                       0

    file                                                   A_probed A_nocontext A_handoff   B_probed B_nocontext B_handoff
    UFNIA/vcc-havoc/vccp-union1.c.6.reint2.smt2                   0           0         0        372        9990     18944
    UFNIA/spec_sharp/textbook-Sum.dll.6.C.TransformQuant_...       0           0         0        432       33090     18688

THE LEVER REACHES ITS TARGET. On both files that produced a per-universal probe
line at all, arm B hands off **18,944** and **18,688** tuples where arm A hands
off **zero**. These are tuples that were joined and then discarded outright on
the shipped arm --- the exact 100.0 %-of-2,139,815 bucket ADR-2113 located --- and
the widened rule turns them into entailed positive replacements. The mechanism
is not inert on the real corpus; it does the thing it was built to do, at scale,
on files it does not convert inside 24 s.

THE 22 ARE NOT ZEROS, and this is the same caveat ADR-2113 had to make about
34 of its 53 cores. The per-universal probe block sits behind a condition the
loop reaches only on certain exits; a file whose loop ends on a time budget
before it prints NOTHING, which is indistinguishable in the output from a file
that handed off nothing. So this table's denominator for the handoff claim is
2, not 24, and it is stated that way rather than as "2 of 24 reached the
target".

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW. It does not show that handing the tuples off is worth
the clock. Both files stayed `unknown` on both arms, and the A/B's raw losses
are timing-shaped (a 1.5 s arm-A `unsat` against a 22.9 s arm-B `unknown` on one
`UFDTLIRA` file). Reaching the target and paying for it are different questions,
and only the second decides whether the lever ships.
