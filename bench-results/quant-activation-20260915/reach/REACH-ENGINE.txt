## THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST, AND IT CONTRADICTS THIS LANE'S OWN A/B

`UFLIA/simplify2/front_end_suite/javafe.ast.LabelStmt.011.smt2` came back
**`unsat` on the ON arm here** -- and in the A/B, the SAME file on the SAME
binary is `unknown` on BOTH arms:

    A/B row:   unknown  24231 ms   unknown  24131 ms   (arm order B first)
    probe run: unknown             UNSAT              (pinned s4 cores 0-7)

Both A/B arms spent the whole 24 s budget. The probe run, on a different core
class with different contention, finished inside it.

**RETRACTED, BY THIS LANE'S OWN RE-CHECK.** The paragraph that stood here read
"the conversion is REAL and it is MARGINAL AGAINST THE CLOCK", on the strength of
ONE observation. `recheck-movers.sh` then ran this file three times per arm on a
pinned core with the arms alternating within the passes, and arm B returned
`unknown` 3 of 3:

    LabelStmt.011   A: unknown unknown unknown   B: unknown unknown unknown
                    -> UNSTABLE

So the single `unsat` does not reproduce, and it was not evidence of a
conversion -- it was one run of a file that sits near the budget, on a different
host and core class, with tracing on. The repository's own rule caught this and
it was MY claim that failed it: *a traced plan's "verified numerically" is itself
a claim -- re-run its numbers, do not inherit them.*

**What survives is narrower and still worth having.** The handoff and
`rej_nocontext` columns below are counters read off a single run, and they move
by three to four orders of magnitude -- 0 to 18,688 handoffs, 33,090 to 0
nocontext. A number that size is not a coin flip between two runs, and it says
the mechanism executes at scale on real files. A VERDICT at the edge of a 24 s
budget is a coin flip between two runs, and this file proved it. The two are
different kinds of measurement and only the first is supported here. Two
consequences follow, and neither is comfortable:

1. **A single 24 s pairing cannot settle a VERDICT on this corpus**, in either
   direction. The re-check bears this out on both sides: of the A/B's three raw
   movers, one is STABLE-GAIN, one is STABLE-LOSS, and one is UNSTABLE -- the
   `UFDTLIRA` "loss" was arm A deciding once and neither arm deciding on any of
   the six re-check passes.
2. **The counters are the durable finding, not the verdicts.** The lever empties
   `rej_nocontext` on the files the corrected sizing predicts, and that is a
   statement about where the remaining cost now is -- the interleaved ground
   closure over a set that is larger than before -- which is the next lane's
   question rather than this one's.
