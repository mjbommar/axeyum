## THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST, AND IT CONTRADICTS THIS LANE'S OWN A/B

`UFLIA/simplify2/front_end_suite/javafe.ast.LabelStmt.011.smt2` came back
**`unsat` on the ON arm here** -- and in the A/B, the SAME file on the SAME
binary is `unknown` on BOTH arms:

    A/B row:   unknown  24231 ms   unknown  24131 ms   (arm order B first)
    probe run: unknown             UNSAT              (pinned s4 cores 0-7)

Both A/B arms spent the whole 24 s budget. The probe run, on a different core
class with different contention, finished inside it.

**So the conversion is REAL and it is MARGINAL AGAINST THE CLOCK.** The ON arm
does reach the refutation on this file; whether it reaches it inside 24 s is
decided by the machine. Two consequences, and neither is comfortable:

1. **The A/B's +1 understates the lever**, and its -2 may equally be clock and
   not capability. Every mover in this lane is at the noise floor for exactly
   this reason, and `recheck-movers.sh` -- three passes per arm with the arms
   alternating WITHIN the passes -- is not a formality here, it is the only
   thing that separates the two.
2. **A single 24 s pairing cannot settle this lever on this corpus.** The
   honest ship position does not change (0 stable losses is the criterion and
   it is not met), but "the lever does nothing" would be the wrong reading of
   a `-1`. The right reading is that it converts at the edge of the budget,
   which is a statement about where the remaining cost is -- the interleaved
   ground closure over a set that is now larger -- and that is the next lane's
   question, not this one's.
